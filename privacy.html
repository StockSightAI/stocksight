Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

StockSight is a free stock research and learning service at stocksightai.com and in the StockSight app. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with us.
  • What you save: your watchlist, portfolio entries, paper-trading portfolio, price alerts, notes, settings, learning progress (XP, lessons, quiz results and streaks) and the leagues you join.
  • Profile photo: only if you upload one.
  • Usage information: Google Analytics records the pages you view and actions such as signing up or opening a stock, along with general information about your device and browser.
  • Error reports: if the site breaks in your browser, we record the error message, the page and your browser type so we can fix it.
  • Security information: we use scrambled (hashed) versions of IP addresses to limit repeated sign-in attempts and to keep the trending list fair.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To send the emails you ask for: password resets, the weekly digest, price-alert emails and weekly league results. Each of these (except password resets) has an unsubscribe link and a switch in Settings.
  • To show your leagues and their leaderboards.
  • To understand which features are used, and to find and fix problems.
  • To keep StockSight secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What other people can see

  • People in your leagues see your first name and last initial, your profile photo, your learning level, your XP for the week and your challenge streak. They never see your email address.
  • Your profile photo is stored at a public web address so it can be shown in your leagues.
  • Anyone with a league's invite link can see the league's name, how many people are in it and the first name of the person who runs it.
  • Community votes and search counts are anonymous.

4. Services we use

These companies help run StockSight and process data on our behalf or provide content. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase: database and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosting.
  • Resend: sending email.
  • Google: Google sign-in and Google Analytics.
  • Yahoo Finance: market data.
  • TradingView: charts shown on some pages.

5. Cookies and local storage

StockSight saves your sign-in session and preferences in your browser's local storage. Google Analytics uses cookies to measure usage, and embedded TradingView charts may set their own cookies.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. Error reports are deleted after 14 days. When you delete your account, we delete your account data, your profile photo and your league memberships.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time from your settings in StockSight. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.
  • Turn emails off in Settings, Notifications, or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Remove your photo in Settings, Overview.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by emailing us.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a unique salt, and are never stored in plain text. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only our server can read account data.

9. Children

StockSight is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Not financial advice

StockSight provides market data, research tools and lessons for information and education only. Nothing on StockSight is financial, investment or legal advice. Paper trading uses pretend money.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in StockSight.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests: stocksightai@gmail.com
